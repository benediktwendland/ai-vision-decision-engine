The 5-Step Selection Framework (Adapted from Werheid et al., WZL RWTH Aachen 2026)

STEP 1

Use Case & Net Present Value (NPV)

Project scope definition, multi-stakeholder governance (Works Council, QA, IT/OT), and dynamic capital budgeting (NPV & hurdle rate).

STEP 2

Requirement Definition

Input of production speed, Field of View (FoV), working distance, smallest defect size, 2D/3D spatial needs, and material properties.

STEP 3

Optics & Sensors

Analytical calculation of Nyquist resolution, exposure limits, and focal length, followed by matching 2D/3D camera, lens, and lighting selection.

STEP 4

AI & Computing Architecture

Dual-dimension co-design: AI model selection (Classical CV, YOLO, PatchCore, FoundationPose) and compute hardware sizing (TOPS vs. VRAM).

STEP 5

Integration & Deployment

4-Pillar deployment verification: OT Data Bus, Optical Calibration, Quality Assurance & drift monitoring, and Operator Guidelines.
