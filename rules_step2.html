2.2 Field of View and Optical Resolution → Lens Focal Length / Zoom, Camera Resolution

Input Variables Deterministic Formula & Active Calculation
Smallest Feature ($d_{\text{min}}$) vs. Whole Object ($\text{FoV}_x \times \text{FoV}_y$) Nyquist Sampling Criterion ($n = 3.0\text{ px}$ per $d_{\text{min}}$):
$R_{\text{min}} = \left(\frac{\text{FoV}_x}{d_{\text{min}}}\cdot 3\right) \times \left(\frac{\text{FoV}_y}{d_{\text{min}}}\cdot 3\right)$
= 0.0 MP
Camera Distance ($\text{WD}$) & Object Width ($\text{FoV}_x$) Thin-Lens Magnification ($w_{\text{sensor}} = 8.8\text{ mm}$):
$f = \text{WD} \cdot \frac{w_{\text{sensor}}}{\text{FoV}_x + w_{\text{sensor}}} \rightarrow$ Nearest Standard C-Mount Lens $[6, 8, 12, 16, 25, 35, 50, 75, 100\text{ mm}]$
= 16 mm
